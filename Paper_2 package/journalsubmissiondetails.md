## Disclosure and scope

The paper proves that the largest nilpotent-free subspace dimension in 3 by 3 matrices over the field with three elements is five. A direct algebraic construction gives the lower bound, while a finite reduction and independently checked search establish the upper bound and refute the proposed six-dimensional construction in this case.

Formal-check scope: The Lean development formalises the reduction and search soundness. Two compiled evaluations remain explicit parts of its trust base. This must not be described as an audit using only the three standard axioms.
