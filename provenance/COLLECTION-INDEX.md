## Upload next to Hexagon

Strongest first: full proofs, then refutations of named conjectures, then unconditional progress. Folders 24 (Erdos 1183) and 35 (Musin 24-cell) are held back. Upload from the `hexagon` folder named.

1. `31 KimberlingConjecture\hexagon\`: The Number of Terms in Greedy Fibonacci–Lucas Representations (proves the Kimberling, Greathouse and Kurkov OEIS conjectures).
2. `23 AndreanovKallusD5\hexagon\`: Densest packings of two translates of a lattice in five dimensions (densest two-translate lattice packings in R^5, the case left open by Andreanov and Kallus).
3. `40 WigdersonConj8F3\hexagon\`: Nilpotent-free spaces of 3×3 matrices over the field with three elements (refutes Wigderson's conjecture (F3, n = 3)).
4. `32 LiuFanoIndexConjecture\hexagon\`: A counterexample to Liu's conjecture on Fano indices in dimension four (refutes Liu's Fano index conjecture).
5. `29 GrundyStrongProduct\hexagon\`: The Grundy domination number is not multiplicative on strong products (refutes the eight-author Grundy strong product conjecture).
6. `33 MaximalSublatticesConvexGeometries\hexagon\`: Maximal sublattices of convex geometries: four counterexamples (refutes the AMSZ hypotheses on convex geometries).
7. `30 H1MaximalSublatticesSD\hexagon\`: Maximal sublattices of finite semidistributive lattices: the complement need not be an interval (answers the AFNS question and refutes the AMSZ conjecture on semidistributive lattices).
8. `37 StarFormingBipartite\hexagon\`: Upper k-star-forming sets and k-independence in bipartite graphs (refutes Sahbi's conjecture, with positive results and NP-completeness).
9. `34 MorleyPSecting\hexagon\`: Regular Morley tetrahedra from p-secting planes (refutes Tran's Morley conjecture).
10. `28 GraffitiDominationCounterexamples\hexagon\`: Counterexamples to five conjectures of Graffiti.pc on domination (refutes five Graffiti.pc domination conjectures).
11. `39 WOWII66ForestNumber\hexagon\`: A counterexample to a conjecture of Graffiti.pc on the forest number (refutes Graffiti.pc 66).
12. `38 UnionClosedLimit\hexagon\`: Attaining the Entropy Limit for Union-Closed Families (union-closed entropy limit).
13. `25 ErdosStrausExceptionalSet\hexagon\`: An explicit exponent for the exceptional set of the Erdős–Straus equation (explicit exponent for the Erdos-Straus exceptional set).
14. `27 ErdosSzekeresHighDim\hexagon\`: New Lower Bounds for Erdős–Szekeres Numbers in Three and Four Dimensions (Erdos-Szekeres lower bounds in high dimension).
15. `36 SpectralAlgebrasCayley\hexagon\`: Modular and integral spectral algebras of abelian Cayley graphs (answers Remark 21.4 of arXiv:2609.09222).
16. `26 ErdosStrausSieveConstant\hexagon\`: A Power-Saving Asymptotic for the Two-Parameter Type II Sieve Sum of the Erdős–Straus Equation (Erdos-Straus sieve constant).
17. `11 MirrorClauseGSC\hexagon\`: The Mirror Clause of the Geometric Selection Conjecture Is Empty (GSC mirror-clause note (a reduction; Deep and Ushashi)).
