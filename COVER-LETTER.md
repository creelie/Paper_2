Editors, Annals of Mathematics

Please consider "Nilpotent-free spaces of 3 x 3 matrices over the field with three elements," by Deep Bhattacharjee, for publication.

The paper determines the maximum dimension of a nilpotent-free subspace of M3(F3): it is five. This gives a counterexample to the universal existence assertion of Conjecture 8 in Yuval Wigderson's "Duality for matrix space questions" (arXiv:2609.29177v1). The Gerstenhaber-Serezhkin theorem itself is unaffected.

The five-dimensional example has a direct algebraic proof. The upper bound reduces to all 3^18 coefficient matrices and is verified by a complete search with 3,109,888 nodes. The manuscript supplies the coverage argument and the full independent C program. The accompanying Lean development formalizes the reduction and soundness; its two compiled evaluations and their additional trust are disclosed explicitly. Characteristic-three structural propositions and examples over F2 and F5 place the obstruction in context.
